Meng Li （李猛）

Assistant Professor
School of Computer Science, Nanjing University

Email: meng@nju.edu.cn
Office: CS Building 312

I am an Assistant Professor at Nanjing University’s School of Computer Science and lead the on-device intelligence subgroup within LANDS. I develop models and systems for On-Device Agents (端侧智能体), making intelligent agents practical on everyday devices.

I received my B.S. (2016) and Ph.D. (2023) from Nanjing University. My doctoral dissertation received outstanding dissertation awards from the Chinese Institute of Electronics (CIE), Jiangsu Province, and Nanjing University. I have published over 40 papers in venues including ISCA, SIGMOD, VLDB, and EuroSys, and held visiting research appointments at Alibaba DAMO Academy and NTU.
